Step 1 · Gemini app

Character sheets

Open gemini.google.com, choose image creation, attach the app art shown (from your Mac at the path under each picture), paste the prompt. Regenerate until it looks right, then download it with the name shown. Every later image uses these, so take your time here.

Steps 2 and 3 · Gemini app, then Flow

The 12 shots

For each shot: in a new Gemini chat, open Tools and make sure video is switched off and image is on, then make the still with the listed sheets attached. Then in Flow, start a Frames to Video clip, add the still as the first frame, paste the video prompt, and use Veo 3.1 Fast for drafts. Download the clip with the name shown.

0 of 12 clips done
Step 4 · Claude

Hand it back

Where to save

Put the clips and the S12-frame.png still in mac-Tamagotchi/film/from-gemini/. If the names get messy, that's fine: tell Claude and it will sort them by looking at each one.

What Claude does

Cuts the 12 clips together at 1080p, smooths the joins, adds the MacTamagotchi end card and English subtitles, and mixes in a music track if you add one.

Credits

Draft every shot in Fast and only redo the best-loved ones in Quality. A shot that goes wrong twice usually needs a simpler action line; ask Claude to rewrite it.

Voices

Veo makes a fresh voice for every clip, so Mochi can sound a little different from shot to shot. Keep the voice line in the prompt word for word and regenerate any clip that sounds off.
